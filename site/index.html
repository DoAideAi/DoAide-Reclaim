Find the money your family forgot.

Old bank accounts, shares, insurance payouts and mutual funds that nobody claimed are held for the family, often for decades. Search for every relative on the official government portals in one evening. Free, with no account.

  • ₹86,917 crorein unclaimed bank deposits with RBI (June 2026)
  • ₹1.84 lakh crorein unclaimed shares and dividends (IEPF Authority estimate)
  • ₹12,700+ crorein unclaimed insurance and mutual funds (PIB, March 2026)

Type PAN, Aadhaar, dates of birth and account numbers only on the official sites. This page never asks for them and has no server storage: your list stays on this device until you clear it.

1. Who should you look for?

Start with relatives who have died, then elders who had bank accounts, shares, LIC policies or mutual funds.

2. Send what you found

These lines say which relatives you searched for, which kinds of details you had and what each site showed. They contain no names, numbers or dates. Send them to whoever shared this page with you.
